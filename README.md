### 🚀 Academic Vision & About Me

I am an AI Engineer and Medical Imaging Researcher based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, bridging the gap between complex machine learning research and robust software engineering. With an MSc in Computer Engineering (AI & Data Engineering), I specialize in designing biologically inspired optimization frameworks to enhance clinical-grade medical image analysis and building end-to-end intelligent systems.

- 🧠 **Current Focus:** Deep learning architectures (U-Net) for medical image segmentation (specifically targeting ischemic stroke lesions via ATLAS 2.0 and LGG datasets) and neuro-inspired optimization algorithms.
- 🎯 **Research Objective:** To develop adaptive learning mechanisms that improve diagnostic accuracy, model robustness, and clinical reliability.
- 💻 **Engineering:** Developing full-stack AI applications and computer vision pipelines for real-time video stream analysis. 
- 🤝 **Collaboration:** Actively seeking fully funded MSc/PhD research positions, Research Assistant (RA) opportunities, and international academic collaborations.

### 🔬 Research Interests

- **Medical Image Analysis:** Brain MRI and CT segmentation, NIfTI handling, and normalization pipelines.
- **Bio-Inspired Optimization:** Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Genetic Algorithms (GA), Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), and Grey Wolf Optimization (GWO).
- **Deep Learning & XAI:** U-Net optimization, hyperparameter evolution, and Explainable AI for healthcare systems.
